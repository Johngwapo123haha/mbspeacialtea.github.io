About Us

Good food, no fuss, no fancy prices.

MB Street Food Corner started in 2024 as a small corner stall with one simple goal: serve the street food and milk tea we grew up loving, made fresh every day and priced so everyone can grab a bite without thinking twice.

From sizzling silog meals in the morning to skewers, fries and milk tea any time of day, every item on our menu is cooked to order. Eat in at our corner, take it to go, or have it delivered — either way, we want it to feel like your regular chill-out spot.

  • Affordable — street food and drinks priced for everyday cravings
  • Fresh — cooked and prepared per order, not sitting under a heat lamp
  • Friendly — a casual corner to eat, chill, and come back to
